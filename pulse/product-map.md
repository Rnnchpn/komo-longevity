# KŌMØ Pulse Personal — Product Map

Status model: `DRAFT → REVIEW → VALIDATED → LOCKED`.

No page moves to `VALIDATED` without explicit founder review. A `LOCKED` page may only change through a deliberate product decision.

## Patient journey

| ID | Page | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | Access / Create profile | Enter Pulse, create account, consent/onboarding shell, start profile | PLANNED |
| 01 | Home | Understand current mobility, next action and whole KŌMØ ecosystem in <30 seconds | REVIEW |
| 02 | My Score | Motion result, domains, Muscle Signature, confidence and detail on demand | PLANNED |
| 03 | My Progress | T0 → M3 → M6 → M12 trajectory and domain comparisons | PLANNED |
| 04 | My Assessments | Upcoming/past assessments, preparation status, reports and follow-up | PLANNED |
| 05 | KŌMØ Motion | Patient-facing explanation of functional assessment and results | PLANNED |
| 06 | KŌMØ Clinical | Patient-facing medical layer, physician interpretation and plan | PLANNED |
| 07 | Network | Find KŌMØ locations / practitioners and understand availability | PLANNED |
| 08 | Ecosystem / Library | Library, Method, Case, Academy/content access according to role | PLANNED |
| 09 | Profile & Documents | Identity/profile completion, preferences, documents and account settings | PLANNED |

## Page 01 — Home validation criteria

The Home is valid only if a patient can answer these questions in less than 30 seconds:

1. What is my current mobility result?
2. Which dimensions are strongest / deserve attention?
3. What are my three priorities?
4. Am I progressing?
5. What is my next KŌMØ step?
6. Where do Motion, Clinical, Network and Library live?
7. How do I complete or update my profile?

### Desktop principle

Editorial and architectural. Large whitespace, a fixed navigation rail, one dominant mobility result, a quiet trajectory, restrained interactions and no medical-dashboard visual language.

### Mobile principle

Native mobile hierarchy rather than compressed desktop. Bottom navigation, one primary action per screen region, horizontally scrollable domains, reduced density, thumb-friendly targets, app-like transitions.

### Non-goals for Page 01

- no raw measurement tables;
- no clinical diagnosis;
- no billing;
- no real authentication yet;
- no identifiable real health data;
- no editable medical data;
- no over-detailed science copy.

## Synthetic-only rule

Until the security/compliance architecture is reviewed, all screenshots, demos and prototypes use synthetic persons and synthetic mobility data only.
