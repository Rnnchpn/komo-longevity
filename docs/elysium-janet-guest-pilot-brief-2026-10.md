# ELYSIUM × Yacht Guests — Janet Pilot Brief (internal)
October 30, 2026 · KŌMØ / ELYSIUM · Cannes–Monaco–Saint-Tropez

## What is being sold
**ELYSIUM is the private service desk for yacht guests, owners and their teams, not an AIS map or an empty superyacht advertising marketplace.**
A yacht guest opens a single-screen mobile web app, selects location and service, and submits a minimal private enquiry. The ELYSIUM desk reviews logistics and returns an actual quote. Availability, medical feasibility and booking are never claimed automatically.

### Customer journey to demonstrate
1. Captain / private assistant opens a unique invitation URL or its QR on the guest's phone: `https://komolongevity.com/elysium/guest/?ref=janet`.
2. Guest chooses Cannes, Monaco or Saint-Tropez, then Motion & Longevity / Wellness / Beauty / Dining / Experiences / Private Concierge.
3. A one-screen request records the guest's name, email, optional yacht and date, role, consent and logistical note — **no health data**.
4. Success screen confirms **request recorded**, never that a service was booked.
5. Owner reviews the request under ELYSIUM Broker Studio > owner-only Private Desk, contacts client, qualifies supplier/availability and finalizes quotation/payment manually.

**Partner/captain specific entry**: `/elysium/guest/?ref=janet&role=captain`. The referral field is not a credential and does not by itself grant a membership or claim a referral fee.

### First commercial shortlist
- **Signature Motion On Board** — non-medical functional assessment and movement follow-up from KŌMØ; initial scalable proprietary service.
- **Wellness & Recovery** — coordinated qualified specialists, only when actually available.
- **Beauty & Aesthetics** — non-medical beauty services; any medical treatment is a *separate independent physician service*, requiring suitability and approvals.
- **Private Dining & Experiences** — access / hospitality partners, quotes after actual availability confirmation.
- **Private Concierge** — local coordination with captain/PA and qualified suppliers.

Do not imply provider exclusivity, 24/7 staffing, signed partnership with specific venues, instant reservation, or AIS-verified yacht boarding.
