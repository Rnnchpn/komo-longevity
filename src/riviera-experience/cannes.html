The format

A private house.
Not a hotel package.

KŌMØ takes over one intimate Riviera villa and turns it into a house for assessment, conversation, creativity, recovery and food.

Guests choose their own nearby hotel or residence. The villa is reserved for the experience itself, keeping the pilot smaller, more exclusive and operationally simple.
