The Mediterranean edition

More space to move.
More time to recover.

The Costa Blanca format keeps the KŌMØ scientific core, but gives the experience more outdoor movement, sea air, food and recovery.

Guests stay independently nearby. The villa acts as a private KŌMØ house for assessment, workshops, meals and debriefs rather than accommodation.

The programme

Measure the body.
Change the rhythm.

Assessment remains precise, while the rest of the retreat is designed around movement, food, sleep, sunlight and recovery.

FRI · SUNSET

Arrival & personal context

Welcome at the villa, lifestyle and recovery profile, goals, sleep, activity, pain and the information required to personalise the weekend.

Baseline · Welcome dinner · Mediterranean rhythm
SAT · EARLY MORNING

Move before measuring

A light mobility or coastal walking session allows the group to begin with movement rather than a clinic-like routine.

Mobility · Breath · Outdoor movement
SAT · MORNING

KŌMØ functional assessment

Movement quality, gait, balance, muscle activation, lower-limb capacity, strength and selected functional markers form the individual baseline.

Motion · Muscle · Strength · Balance
SAT · AFTERNOON

Recovery & lifestyle ateliers

Nutrition, body composition, sleep, skin, creative practice and restorative sessions are delivered in small groups. Medical options remain separate and individually assessed.

Nutrition · Sleep · Skin · Recovery · Art
SAT · NIGHT

Private Mediterranean dinner

A chef-led table focused on conversation and pleasure, with the scientific agenda deliberately put away for the evening.

Food · Culture · Social connection
SUN · MORNING

Trajectory session

Results are translated into a practical plan: what to preserve, what to improve and what to reassess over the next 90 days.

Debrief · Priorities · Personal roadmap
