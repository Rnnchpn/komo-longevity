KŌMØ Experience

A private house.
Not a hotel.

For three days, KŌMØ takes over one remarkable place on the Riviera and turns it into an intimate house for measurement, conversation, creativity, recovery and food.

Guests stay where they choose nearby. No bedrooms, no resort logistics, no forced retreat format. The house is dedicated to the experience itself — quiet, personal and deliberately small.

The three-day rhythm

Arrive. Measure.
Understand.

We keep the structure clear and the pace unhurried. The scientific work happens alongside the things that make a weekend worth remembering.

02 · Saturday morning · Measure

Your body
in motion.

The core KŌMØ assessment brings together functional movement, gait, balance, muscular activation and body composition. The objective is not more data. It is a useful picture of capacity.

The assessment protocol is led by the KŌMØ team and adapted to each participant.

Saturday afternoon · Explore

Longevity,
lived rather than lectured.
