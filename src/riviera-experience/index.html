The KŌMØ method

This is not a spa.
This is a trajectory.

KŌMØ uses functional and muscular assessment, movement analysis and selected lifestyle or biological signals to create a useful picture of how a guest moves and performs today — then follows how that picture changes over time.

01

Understand

Context, goals, sleep, stress, activity, pain and recovery.

02

Measure

Mobility, gait, balance, muscular activation, strength and function.

03

Translate

Motion Score, functional profile and a short list of priorities.

04

Follow

Repeat the same signals and measure the direction of change.
