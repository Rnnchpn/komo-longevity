KŌMØ Yachting Retreat

The yacht becomes
a private longevity laboratory.

KŌMØ brings a new generation of longevity assessment directly onboard: a portable, data-driven experience designed to understand how the body moves, performs and evolves over time.

Built around one simple principle — movement is one of the most powerful markers of healthy ageing — KŌMØ combines functional assessment, muscle analysis and selected lifestyle or biological data to create a precise picture of each guest’s current physical trajectory.

No clinic. No cumbersome equipment. No interruption to the journey.

A personalised longevity journey

Not simply how fit you are today.
How well you are likely to move tomorrow.

Each guest follows an individual assessment pathway designed to understand strength, mobility, balance, coordination, recovery and functional resilience. The experience starts with personal context and lifestyle before moving into KŌMØ’s functional and muscular analysis.

Multiple physiological and functional markers are brought together into a clear, actionable overview. Results are translated into an individual Motion profile, functional interpretation and personalised longevity trajectory that can extend well beyond the voyage.

How the voyage works

Integrated into the journey.
Never imposed on it.

The onboard format is modular. Assessment is scheduled around the charter rhythm, and the rest of the programme can be selected according to the guest profile and itinerary.

PHASE 01

Private profile

Goals, lifestyle, previous data, sleep, stress, recovery, pain and activity are reviewed discreetly before or at the beginning of the voyage.

KŌMØ onboard assessment
