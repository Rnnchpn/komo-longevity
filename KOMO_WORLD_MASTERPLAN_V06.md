# KŌMØ World · Masterplan V0.6

## North star

KŌMØ World must feel like a real place before it feels like software.

The player must understand the world within seconds of spawning: where they are, what the main building is, where their Functional Digital Twin lives, what is currently accessible, and what remains to be unlocked.

The spatial language borrows only interaction principles from welcoming sandbox/MMORPG worlds: immediate spawn legibility, iconic central landmark, visible routes, gradual discovery and unlockable frontiers. The visual identity remains distinctly KŌMØ: premium, calm, scientific, Mediterranean and contemporary.

## World topology

### Layer 0 · The World Map

KŌMØ World is represented as an expandable planisphere. The first playable landmass is the **Core Island**. Additional geographic districts exist beyond a visible frontier but remain locked until real KŌMØ partners, experiences or programmes are available.

Future districts can include:
- Riviera
- Paris / Locotech
- Jávea
- Ibiza
- Capri
- Dubai
- Performance destinations
- Recovery destinations

Locked areas are not hidden. They are visible as future geography behind an elegant boundary. This creates anticipation without pretending that content already exists.

## Spawn sequence

### 1. KŌMØ Arrival Point

Every first session begins at a consistent spawn marker on the Arrival Plaza.

The initial camera composition must show:
- the player's full avatar;
- the main KŌMØ Hall directly ahead;
- the Reception Desk through the entrance axis;
- a readable KŌMØ World landmark;
- one locked frontier visible in the distance;
- no overwhelming interface panels.

### 2. Arrival Plaza

A calm landscaped forecourt acts as the tutorial without looking like a tutorial.

Environmental guidance:
- floor line leading to the Hall;
- subtle signs for Twin Lab, Rehab, Library, Amphitheatre and World Gate;
- a world-map sculpture or floor inlay;
- contextual prompts only when the player approaches an interaction.

## The KŌMØ Hall

The Hall is the persistent central landmark and the main orientation point.

### Grand Atrium

Large central volume with a clear visual axis. The Atrium links all primary destinations.

Primary functions:
- orientation;
- current world state;
- access to the main rooms;
- return point after other districts;
- social gathering space later.

### KŌMØ Desk

A real reception counter sits on the entrance axis.

The Desk can surface:
- Motion Score;
- Motion Age;
- current Twin priority;
- suggested next destination;
- KŌMØ Points / Level;
- World events;
- available partner destinations;
- onboarding help;
- controls/settings.

Future role: Personal KŌMØ Concierge Agent.

## Room 01 · Functional Digital Twin Lab

This is the first major room and the scientific heart of World.

Central object: the user's Functional Digital Twin.

Data streams:
- Myodev;
- functional tests;
- gait;
- strength;
- posture;
- wearables;
- sleep;
- activity;
- Rehab;
- Motion Score.

Core interactions:
- inspect a source;
- inspect provenance;
- focus a body region;
- move through longitudinal snapshots;
- compare baseline / intermediate / today;
- ask KŌMØ AI to explain or navigate the Twin;
- route directly to an authorised Rehab programme.

## Room 02 · KŌMØ Gym / Rehab

Accessible from the Atrium.

The room will later contain specialised zones and a Coach Agent. The Coach receives the minimum authorised Twin context and can guide an existing programme without silently rewriting clinical truth.

## Room 03 · Library

A real walkable library with shelves, objects, citations and a Librarian Agent.

## Room 04 · Amphitheatre

Persistent event space for KŌMØ Talks, Locotech, expert sessions and replays.

## Room 05 · Store

Inventory, KŌMØ Life, avatar cosmetics, memberships and eligible real-world products/experiences. No pay-to-win health mechanics.

## World Gate · Unlockable Frontier

Beyond the Hall lies the **World Gate**.

### Initial state

**LOCKED — RIVIERA DISTRICT FORMING**

The player can approach the boundary and see silhouettes, lights or a distant map of future locations, but cannot cross it.

### Unlock contract

A district becomes open only when its minimum real-world content contract is satisfied. Example Riviera requirements:
- at least one validated KŌMØ partner Node;
- partner profile and location data;
- at least one actionable experience or booking route;
- commercial and privacy permissions ready;
- world representation approved.

The unlock is therefore tied to real operational progress, not a fake game milestone.

### Narrative

The boundary should evolve visually:
1. Closed horizon / frosted field.
2. First partner signal appears.
3. Gate partially powers on.
4. District map becomes readable.
5. Frontier opens.

## Player control system

Controls belong to the player and must never be hard-coded as the only option.

### Desktop remappable actions

- Move Forward
- Move Backward
- Move Left
- Move Right
- Sprint
- Interact
- Open World Map
- Open KŌMØ AI
- Open Settings
- Reset Camera

Default profiles:
- WASD
- ZQSD
- Arrow Keys
- custom

Every binding is persisted per user/device.

### Camera settings

- horizontal sensitivity;
- vertical sensitivity;
- invert vertical axis;
- camera distance;
- camera smoothing;
- optional auto-center;

### Mobile

- analog movement joystick;
- right-side free-look zone;
- selectable joystick size;
- selectable joystick opacity;
- selectable look sensitivity;
- invert vertical axis;
- optional left-handed mode;
- optional fixed/dynamic joystick;
- sprint/action buttons later.

### Accessibility

Planned:
- reduced camera motion;
- larger interaction prompts;
- high contrast navigation mode;
- one-handed mobile layout;
- gamepad support;
- keyboard-only navigation.

## Interaction language

No permanent forest of buttons. World objects reveal actions only when the player is close enough.

Examples:
- `E · Talk to KŌMØ Desk`
- `E · Enter Twin Lab`
- `E · Inspect World Gate`
- `LOCKED · Riviera District`

On mobile the same interaction becomes a contextual Action button.

## Visual direction

### Core Island

- Mediterranean stone / warm mineral surfaces;
- sage vegetation;
- water or reflective horizon;
- sand / limestone secondary tones;
- dark bronze details;
- controlled glass;
- large, calm architectural volumes;
- minimal signage;
- dusk / golden-hour capable lighting.

### Hall

- monumental but welcoming;
- hotel / museum / research-campus crossover;
- strong central axis;
- high ceiling;
- sculptural light;
- not clinical-white;
- not gaming-neon;
- not generic SaaS.

### Digital Twin Lab

Darker, more technical, but still architectural. The Twin is treated like an exhibit of living longitudinal data rather than a dashboard.

## Build sequence before Astra

1. Spawn + Arrival Plaza.
2. Monumental Hall exterior and entrance axis.
3. Reception Desk and contextual interaction.
4. Twin Lab entrance and first complete room.
5. Locked World Gate / Frontier.
6. World map / planisphere object.
7. Full player-control settings and persistence.
8. Mobile twin-stick polish.
9. Basic proximity interactions.
10. Environment polish: materials, lighting, vegetation, signage, spatial audio placeholders.
11. Rehab shell.
12. Library / Amphitheatre / Store shells.

Only then should an Astra-class spatial agent be invited to iterate on geometry, art direction, environment coherence, QA and content expansion.

## Core product rule

**Astra should inherit a world with rules, not invent the rules of the world.**
