# KŌMØ Arena · Product & Gameplay V0.1

## Purpose

KŌMØ Arena is the immediate social-retention layer of KŌMØ World. It gives users a reason to return even when they are not reviewing their Functional Digital Twin or completing a rehabilitation session.

The Arena must feel competitive and alive without turning health status, disability, biological age, Motion Score or clinical deficit into a public hierarchy.

## Core principle

Compete on participation, execution and challenge completion — never on being healthier than someone else.

## Location

A major hall accessible from The KŌMØ Hall, large enough to become a visible landmark and one of the first destinations new players notice after onboarding.

## Main modes

### Daily Arena
Short 3–8 minute challenges renewed daily.
Examples:
- timed mobility sequence;
- reaction / coordination course;
- balance challenge;
- step rhythm challenge;
- movement precision challenge;
- knowledge + movement hybrid challenge.

### Weekly League
A rotating set of challenges with cumulative points over seven days.

### Seasonal League
4–8 week seasons with themes, cosmetics, trophies, badges and club competition.

### Club Arena
KŌMØ Clubs compete on safe collective variables such as:
- sessions completed;
- challenge completion rate;
- participation streaks;
- attendance;
- team objectives;
- cooperative milestones.

### Live Events
Scheduled events in the Arena tied to KŌMØ, Locotech, partner venues or special launches.

## Leaderboards

Allowed leaderboard variables:
- Arena Points;
- challenge completion time where the challenge is designed as a game and not a medical test;
- accuracy / execution score for game-specific tasks;
- streaks;
- sessions completed;
- season XP;
- team / club contribution;
- cooperative milestones.

Forbidden public leaderboard variables:
- Motion Score;
- Motion Age;
- diagnosis;
- disability status;
- pain score;
- measured weakness;
- asymmetry;
- gait impairment;
- rehab outcome;
- medical or clinical status.

A user's Functional Digital Twin may privately adapt challenge difficulty or recommend an appropriate Arena mode, but the underlying health data stays private.

## Economy

Arena rewards can include:
- KŌMØ Points;
- XP / Level progression;
- cosmetic items;
- Arena banners;
- seasonal badges;
- KŌMØ Life digital outfits;
- access to special events;
- partner non-medical perks when lawful.

No Arena purchase may increase Motion Score or create a clinical advantage.

## Social systems

- friends;
- parties;
- clubs;
- spectators;
- emotes;
- podiums;
- seasonal champions;
- public event boards;
- private / friends-only leaderboards;
- opt-out from public ranking.

## Arena Agent

The Arena Host Agent can:
- explain rules;
- match users into suitable challenges;
- introduce events;
- comment on gameplay;
- surface personal progress;
- present season standings;
- coordinate group challenges.

It cannot expose private Twin data to other players or publicly explain why a user was matched to a specific difficulty.

## Data separation

Twin data = private truth layer.
Arena data = game / social layer.

The bridge between them is permissioned and minimal:
- eligibility category;
- challenge difficulty band;
- restrictions required for safety;
- completion result.

No raw Myodev, clinical, sleep or medical data enters public Arena presence payloads.
