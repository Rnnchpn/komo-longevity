The Concept

Longevity is more than adding years.
It’s how we move through them.

KŌMØ began with a medical question: how do we understand and preserve locomotor longevity? KŌMØ Life takes that question outside the consultation room. It translates the pursuit of a longer, more mobile life into clothing, objects, stories and experiences that can be lived every day.

Science remains underneath. Culture is what makes it visible, desirable and shared.

Culture in Motion

At the intersection of science, design and lived experience.

KŌMØ Life is where medical thinking meets the things that shape a life: what we wear, where we go, how we recover, what we read and the people we meet. We will work with minds across medicine, movement, design, hospitality and the arts — not to medicalise culture, but to make longevity part of it.
